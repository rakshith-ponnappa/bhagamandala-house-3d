### Exposed Laterite Wall Treatment — Kodagu Ainmane Heritage

All three bedrooms (Master GF, BR2 GF, FF Master) feature **exposed laterite stone walls** (ಜಂಬಿಟ್ ಕಲ್ಲು) with **linseed oil finish** — a heritage design choice honoring the Kodagu Gowda Ainmane tradition.

**Why Exposed Laterite in Bedrooms:**
- **Heritage:** Traditional Kodagu Ainmane (ancestral homes) always used exposed laterite — warm, earthy, nostalgic
- **Durability:** No repainting every 3-5 years. Laterite lasts generations with minimal maintenance
- **Climate:** Laterite breathes — absorbs/releases humidity naturally (ideal for 5,000mm/year rainfall zone)
- **Cost:** Saves ~₹2.5–3L by eliminating plaster + paint on ~1,200 sqft of bedroom wall area
- **Thermal:** Natural insulation — cool in summer, warm in winter

**Linseed Oil Treatment Process:**
1. After masonry cures (28 days), brush clean all dust from laterite surface
2. Apply raw linseed oil (Alsi Tel / ಅಗಸೆ ಎಣ್ಣೆ) with wide brush — 2 coats, 24hr gap
3. Final coat: boiled linseed oil for faster drying and richer sheen
4. Re-apply once every 3-5 years (vs repainting which costs ₹30-50K each time)
5. Result: Warm terracotta-red satin finish that deepens with age

**Which walls are exposed laterite:**
- All interior bedroom walls (4 walls per room × 3 rooms)
- Ceiling remains white matte paint (plastered RCC slab)
- Other rooms (hall, kitchen, baths, passage) remain plastered + painted as specified above
